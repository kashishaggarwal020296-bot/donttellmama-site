"Rooftop" has become a word that gets attached to almost any bar with
outdoor seating. Not all of them earn it. Before you pick where to spend a
Friday night, here's what's actually worth checking for.

## The view is the easy part

Most rooftops in Bangalore have some kind of skyline or greenery to look at.
That's table stakes, not the thing that makes a night good. The real
differences show up once you're actually there.

## What separates a good one from an average one

- **Weather resilience.** Bangalore's rooftop weather is unpredictable —
  evening showers are common for a good chunk of the year. A rooftop that's
  entirely open-air with no cover is one unlucky evening away from being
  cancelled. Ours is partly covered, partly open — the energy of a rooftop
  without the gamble.
- **Live music that's actually live.** A lot of places run a playlist and
  call it ambience. If a venue advertises live music, check which nights —
  ours runs it Fridays.
- **A bar program with a point of view.** Anyone can serve a mojito. Look
  for at least one or two house cocktails you can't get elsewhere — that's
  usually a sign the kitchen and bar care about more than turning tables.
- **Capacity for groups without losing the room's feel.** The best rooftops
  can hold a big weekend crowd on a Saturday and a quiet Sunday lunch table
  the next day, without either feeling wrong for the space.
- **A kitchen that isn't an afterthought.** Rooftop bars built purely around
  drinks tend to have forgettable food. Ones worth returning to usually have
  a real menu — not just fries and nachos as damage control.

## How we think about it, from the inside

Don't Tell Mama sits above HSR Layout and has run since 2010, which in
Bangalore's bar scene is long enough to have seen most trends come and go.
The format that's lasted: good cocktails, a kitchen worth ordering from
properly (see the [full food menu](/food-menu.html)), and nights that change
through the week instead of running the same format every night — ladies'
cocktails midweek, live music Fridays, a long table on Saturdays, a slower
pace on Sundays.

> A rooftop is just a room with a view until the music, the drinks and the
> crowd give it a reason to come back.
